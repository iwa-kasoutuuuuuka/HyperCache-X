# HyperCache X User Manual (English Edition)

## 1. Overview
HyperCache X is a sophisticated storage tool that transforms your slow HDD performance to near-SSD levels. By intelligently caching your most active data blocks onto a small portion of your SSD, it ensures a faster, smoother computing experience.

---

## 2. Quick Start Guide
### Step 1: Launch
Right-click `HCX.App.exe` and select **"Run as Administrator"**. Administrator privileges are strictly required for kernel-level speed adjustments.

### Step 2: Driver Setup
Locate the "Engine Configuration" card on the left side of the dashboard. Click **[Setup Driver]**. The status indicator will turn Green when the kernel driver is successfully attached.

### Step 3: Deployment
1. Select your target HDD volume (e.g., C: or D: drive).
2. Select your high-speed SSD cache drive.
3. Click **[Start Engine]**. Acceleration will commence immediately.

---

## 3. Dashboard Features
- **Throughput Monitor**: Displays real-time data flow in MB/s.
- **Hit Rate**: Indicates what percentage of requests are being served from the high-speed cache.
- **AI Score**: Shows the intelligence level of current data placement. A higher score means more optimal caching.
- **Heatmap**: Visualization of block activity on your physical volume.

---

## 4. Background and Tray Usage
HyperCache X is designed to work silently in the background:
- **Minimize/Close**: When you click the [X] button, the app stays active in the system tray.
- **Tray Menu**: Right-click the Shield icon in the tray to stop acceleration or completely exit the app.
- **System Safety**: Always use the **[Stop]** button before shutting down your PC or removing drives to ensure all cached data is synchronized.

---

## 5. Simulation Mode
If you do not have the kernel driver installed, the app will run in **Simulation Mode**. This allows you to explore the interface and see the AI engine's behavior using virtual data.
